Tips for a good viva

The same tips work for the real exam.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge and allow microphone access.
  2. Sit in a quiet place and speak clearly at a normal speed.
  3. Explain in your own words. Mention the key terms from your report.
  4. Check the live transcript before submitting. You can re-record once.
