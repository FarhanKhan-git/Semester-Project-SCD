How VivaVoice works

Four steps from document to traceable results.

1

Upload

The examiner uploads a PDF or DOCX and sets the number of questions, difficulty and time.

2

Share the code

VivaVoice prepares the document and creates a unique exam code for the class.

3

Voice viva

The AI agent speaks each question. The student answers out loud and sees a live transcript.

4

Review

The examiner sees scores, feedback, transcripts and sources, and can override any score.

Built for fair oral exams

Designed around the needs of both examiners and students.

Grounded questions

Every question is generated from one section of your document. Nothing from outside the syllabus.

Spoken exam

Questions are read aloud and answers are captured by voice, with a typing fallback.

Meaning-based grading

Each answer is scored against its source section with a short reason.

Different questions per student

Each student gets questions from different parts of the document at the same difficulty.

Integrity flags

Tab switches and window changes during the exam are recorded for the examiner.

Practice mode

Students upload their own report and rehearse a viva before the real one.
