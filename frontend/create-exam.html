What happens next

After you click Create Exam:

  1. The text is extracted from your document.
  2. It is split into small sections (chunks) and stored.
  3. Each student gets questions from different sections at the same difficulty.
  4. Answers are graded against the section each question came from.

Grounding rules

The examiner agent never asks anything that is not in your document, does not give hints, and shows you the source of every question in the results.
